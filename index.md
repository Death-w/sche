1. **Keep a small notebook** at your desk. Every day, write the date, subjects studied, and one thing you didn't fully understand — revisit it the next day.
2. **Physics Theory → same-day Questions rule:** Never end the day without practising at least 5 Physics or Math questions related to what you studied that evening.

## 📌 GENERAL RULES FOR THE ENTIRE PLAN

1. **Physics & Math always get 2 slots per day** — one theory, one questions. This is non-negotiable. These are your weaker subjects and consistent daily practice is the most effective way to improve.
2. **Never skip the outdoor play break.** Physical activity refreshes your brain and improves memory consolidation. It is part of the study strategy.
3. **Don't study during dinner or rest time.** These recovery windows protect your focus for the evening session.
4. **Modify freely — but maintain the two-slot rule for Physics & Math.** You can swap subjects between days based on school load, upcoming tests, or energy levels.
5. **Quality over quantity.** 50 focused minutes beats 90 distracted minutes. If you're not concentrating, take a 5-minute break rather than pushing through.
